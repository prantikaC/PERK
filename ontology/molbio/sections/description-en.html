PERKOnto-MolBio: Molecular Biology extension of PERKOnto: Description back to ToC

A domain extension of PERKOnto for the professional knowledge of a molecular biologist as extractable from personal email communications. It reuses the PERKOnto backbone and specialises the scientific layer into experimental-biology concepts (BiologicalEntity/BiologicalSample/ExperimentalTechnique/Readout/ResearchProblem) plus supporting concepts (Reagent, Instrument, Protocol).
