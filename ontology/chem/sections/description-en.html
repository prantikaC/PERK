PERKOnto-Chem: Computational Chemistry extension of PERKOnto: Description back to ToC

A domain extension of PERKOnto for the professional knowledge of a computational/theoretical chemist as extractable from personal email communications. It reuses the PERKOnto communication, actor, venue and publication-lifecycle backbone and specialises the scientific layer (Dataset/Method/Metric/Task) into chemistry concepts (ChemicalSystem/ComputationalMethod/Observable/ResearchProblem) plus supporting concepts (ForceField, Software, LevelOfTheory).
