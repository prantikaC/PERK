PERKOnto-Grav: Gravitational Physics extension of PERKOnto: Description back to ToC

A domain extension of PERKOnto for the professional knowledge of a gravitational-wave / relativity physicist as extractable from personal email communications. It reuses the PERKOnto backbone and specialises the scientific layer into gravitational-physics concepts (AstrophysicalSource/ObservationalData/AnalysisMethod/PhysicalParameter/ResearchProblem) plus supporting concepts (Detector, WaveformModel, AnalysisPipeline).
